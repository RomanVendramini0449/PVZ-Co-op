PvZ Co-op - how to play
=======================

Both players need:
  - their own Plants vs. Zombies (the Steam Game of the Year edition), and
  - this exact zip. Co-op refuses to connect two different builds, so if one
    of you updates, both of you update.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere. Easiest: unzip it INTO your Plants vs. Zombies
   folder (the one with PlantsVsZombies.exe and main.pak) and the launcher
   finds the game by itself. Anywhere else, it looks in your Steam libraries;
   if it still cannot find the game, press Browse and pick PlantsVsZombies.exe.

2. Double-click PVZ-Coop-Launcher.bat.

3. One of you picks "Host a game" (and the difficulty), the other picks
   "Join a game" and types the host's address. Press Start on both.
   The host can change the difficulty later from the main menu's
   DIFFICULTY button, between levels.

Difficulties
  Normal     the game as it comes
  Hard       each sun worth 15 instead of 25, zombies 1.2x tougher
  Wild       normal sun, every zombie 1x to 2x tougher (rolled per zombie)
  Horde      1.5x as many zombies, each 1x to 2x tougher
  Nightmare  Hard's sun, 1.5x as many zombies, each 1.2x to 2x tougher

Sun is your own. Every sun the game makes comes once for each of you. Your
friend's suns are greyed out and you cannot pick them up; their total shows
under the Menu button. Seed cooldowns are your own too. Money is shared.

Playing over the internet: the host's port (1337 unless you changed it) must
be reachable - forward it on the router, or both join the same virtual LAN
(Tailscale, ZeroTier, Radmin VPN) and use that address.

Trouble connecting? 1-HOST-nettest.bat and 2-JOIN-nettest.bat test the
connection without starting the game. 3-SELFTEST.bat checks the build.
Saves live in %APPDATA%\io.github.wszqkzqk\PvZPortable, not in the game
folder, so your normal PvZ save is never touched.
