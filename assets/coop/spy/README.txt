Spy art goes here
=================

  walk\        numbered PNG frames: walking, looping. FACING LEFT.
  uncloak\     numbered PNG frames: appearing out of thin air (optional;
               without it he fades in)
  attack\      numbered PNG frames: stabbing / eating, looping (optional)

How he plays: he walks in invisible (a faint shimmer), like a Digger
tunnelling, and plants can't hit him; at the far left of the lawn he
uncloaks and turns round, attacking your plants from behind. The game
mirrors him for that, so draw him facing left only.

Same canvas size for every frame; drawn 135 px tall. Then run
python tools\zombie-art\pack_coop_assets.py  and BUILD.bat.
Until then he is a Digger (unseen while cloaked).
