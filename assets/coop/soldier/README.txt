Soldier art goes here
=====================

  walk\        numbered PNG frames: walking, looping. FACING LEFT.
  attack\      numbered PNG frames: the shovel swing, looping (used while
               he is eating a plant)

How he plays: a Buckethead with more health (his helmet takes the hits a
bucket would, then the rest of him). Same canvas size for every frame;
drawn 135 px tall. Then run  python tools\zombie-art\pack_coop_assets.py
and BUILD.bat. Until then he looks like a Buckethead.
